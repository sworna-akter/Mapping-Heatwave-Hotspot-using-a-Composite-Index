# Data

This folder contains the datasets and supporting spatial data used to develop the district-level Heatwave Vulnerability Index (HVI) for Bangladesh.

### Data Sources

The study used the following datasets:

- **MODIS (2013–2022):** Land Surface Temperature (LST) data for measuring heat exposure.
- **Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS, 2011 and 2016):** Demographic and socioeconomic indicators, including population density, age composition, poverty, illiteracy, disability, and unemployment.
- **Sentinel-2:** Vegetation cover, surface water, and built-up area indicators.
- **NASA VIIRS:** Average nighttime light data.
- **LGED:** District administrative boundary shapefiles for spatial mapping and analysis.


### Data Availability

Some source datasets, particularly census and socioeconomic data, may be subject to access restrictions. Raw or restricted datasets are therefore not redistributed in this repository. Data availability is subject to the original providers' terms and permissions.
