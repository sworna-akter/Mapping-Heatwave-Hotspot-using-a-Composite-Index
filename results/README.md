# Results

This folder presents the major findings and visual outputs of the district-level Heatwave Vulnerability Index (HVI) assessment across Bangladesh.

### Key Findings

- **Spatial variation:** Heatwave vulnerability varied considerably across the 64 districts, with higher HVI values concentrated mainly in central and western Bangladesh.
- **High-vulnerability areas:** Dhaka and surrounding districts, including Narayanganj, Gazipur, Manikganj, and Tangail, emerged as important areas of concern.
- **Component relationships:** Exposure and sensitivity showed positive relationships with HVI, while adaptive capacity showed a negative relationship.
- **Spatial clustering:** Local Moran’s I and Getis-Ord Gi* identified statistically significant clusters and hotspots of vulnerability, particularly in central Bangladesh.
- **GWR results:** The relationships between vulnerability and its component indicators varied geographically across districts.
- **Sensitivity analysis:** District vulnerability rankings remained broadly stable under different weighting scenarios.
- **Uncertainty analysis:** Monte Carlo simulation confirmed the robustness of the central high-vulnerability belt, with Dhaka and several surrounding districts consistently ranking among the most vulnerable.

The results highlight substantial geographical inequalities in heatwave vulnerability and the importance of geographically targeted adaptation and resilience planning.
